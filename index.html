How to use

  • Tap the buttons or type on your keyboard, then press = or Enter.
  • Use ( ) for grouping and xʸ for powers, e.g. 2^3 or sin(30).
  • Trig functions use degrees; press DEG to switch to radians.
  • C (or Esc) clears everything, ⌫ deletes the last character.
